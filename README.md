# My Favorite Series

## Stranger Things

**Stranger Things** is a sci-fi horror series set in the 1980s about a group of kids in the small town of *Hawkins, Indiana* who uncover secret experiments and a dark dimension called the **Upside Down**.

### Quick Facts
- **Genre:** Science Fiction, Horror, Drama
- **Platform:** Netflix
- **Premiered:** 2016
- **Creators:** The Duffer Brothers

### Main Characters
1. Eleven
2. Mike Wheeler
3. Dustin Henderson
4. Lucas Sinclair
5. Will Byers

### Why I Like It
> "Friends don't lie."

I like it because of its **mystery**, 80s nostalgia, and strong story about friendship and courage.

### Show Highlights
| Feature | Description |

| Special Power | Telekinesis |
